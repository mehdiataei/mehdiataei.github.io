About

I'm a Principal AI Research Scientist at Autodesk Research. I build simulation‑based synthetic data pipelines and apply them to develop models that reason about physics and engineering design. My focus is on the development of systems for multimodal reasoning, 3D shape generation, and assembly design. I earned a Ph.D. in Computational Physics from the University of Toronto and a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from Sharif University of Technology. I teach AI and machine learning at the University of Toronto and York University.

Research Interests

  • Synthetic Data for Multimodal/Physical Reasoning - Scalable simulation and dataset generation for VLMs and agents
  • Generative Design for Mechanical Assemblies - Models and workflows for automated design exploration
  • Differentiable Physics & HPC - Training and large-scale simulation with JAX/Warp; creator of XLB
